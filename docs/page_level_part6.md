# NOVAAACODE PORTFOLIO

# Volume 3 — Experience Architecture & Page-Level UX

## Part 6 — About, Services, Contact & Footer Experience

---

# 463. Part 6 Objective

The previous parts established:

```text
Identity
↓
Work
↓
Capabilities
↓
Process
↓
Case Studies
```

Part 6 defines the human and commercial side of Novaaacode.

The visitor now needs to understand:

```text
WHO IS BEHIND THE WORK?
        +
WHAT CAN THEY BUILD FOR ME?
        +
HOW DO I START A PROJECT?
```

The pages covered are:

```text
/about
/services
/contact
```

plus:

```text
Homepage Final CTA
Global Footer
```

These experiences must preserve the same editorial design language established throughout the portfolio.

---

# 464. About Page Philosophy

The About page must not open with:

```text
Hi 👋
I'm Ahmad.

I'm a passionate full-stack developer
who loves building amazing digital
experiences...
```

This is explicitly rejected.

It sounds interchangeable with thousands of developer portfolios.

The About page should instead introduce:

```text
Perspective
+
Approach
+
Capability
+
Personality
```

---

# 465. About Page Purpose

The Work pages prove:

```text
WHAT
```

Novaaacode can create.

The About page explains:

```text
WHO
+
HOW
+
WHY
```

behind that work.

```text
Services are displayed as four generic cards.

Every service section uses identical composition.
```

# 583. Contact Failure Conditions

Reject if:

```text
The form contains unnecessary fields.

Budget is forced without an unknown option.

Errors appear aggressively while typing.

CAPTCHA dominates the experience.

Submission has no loading state.

Failure provides no alternative contact method.

Success redirects to a generic page.

The contact experience feels like enterprise lead capture.
```

# 584. Final CTA Failure Conditions

```text
It looks like a small newsletter banner.

There are multiple competing actions.

The text is generic marketing language.

Heavy animation delays the Contact route.

The CTA is visually weaker than the preceding Process section.
```

```text
The footer contains dozens of links.

A newsletter form is added without purpose.

The footer becomes another huge content section.

Large decorative animation runs continuously.
```
